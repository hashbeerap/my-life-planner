## 📱 HOW TO INSTALL AS APP:

### **On iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open in Safari
2. Tap Share button (bottom center)
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. **App appears on home screen!** 🎉

### **On Android:**
1. Open in Chrome
2. Tap menu (3 dots)
3. Tap "Add to Home screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. **App appears on home screen!** 🎉

### **Result:**
- Opens like any app
- No browser bar
- Full screen
- App icon
- Native experience!
